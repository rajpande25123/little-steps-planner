BUILD STRENGTH, KEEP RECOVERING

My weekly gym plan ♡

Six training days (Monday–Saturday) in your 5–7 PM gym window. Sunday is the only full rest day.

Simple rules: warm up for 5–10 minutes, use a weight you can control, stop if you feel sharp pain, and add weight only when all reps feel solid. Ask a trainer to check your form.
MONUpper body A
  • Chest press — 3 × 8–12
  • Lat pulldown — 3 × 8–12
  • Seated cable row — 2 × 10–12
  • Shoulder press — 2 × 8–12
  • Triceps pushdown — 2 × 10–15
  • Biceps curl — 2 × 10–15

Rest 60–120 sec between sets.

TUELower body A
  • Goblet squat or leg press — 3 × 8–12
  • Romanian deadlift — 3 × 8–10
  • Leg curl — 2 × 10–15
  • Calf raise — 3 × 12–15
  • Plank — 3 × 20–45 sec

Keep the movement controlled.

WEDBack + rear delts + core
  • Lat pulldown — 3 × 8–12
  • Chest-supported row — 3 × 8–12
  • Face pulls — 2 × 12–15
  • Rear-delt fly — 2 × 12–15
  • Hammer curls — 2 × 10–15
  • Dead bug — 3 × 10 each side

Train with controlled reps; this is a regular gym day.

THUUpper body B
  • Incline dumbbell press — 3 × 8–12
  • Assisted pull-up / pulldown — 3 × 8–12
  • One-arm dumbbell row — 2 × 10 each side
  • Lateral raise — 2 × 12–15
  • Triceps extension — 2 × 10–15
  • Hammer curl — 2 × 10–15

Prioritize good form over heavier weights.

FRILower body B
  • Leg press / squat — 3 × 8–12
  • Hip thrust — 3 × 8–12
  • Leg extension — 2 × 10–15
  • Leg curl — 2 × 10–15
  • Calf raise — 2 × 12–15
  • Dead bug — 2 × 10 each side

Use a comfortable range of motion.

SATLight full body
  • Chest-supported row — 2 × 10–12
  • Push-ups / chest press — 2 × 8–12
  • Bodyweight squat — 2 × 12
  • Farmer carry — 3 short walks
  • Easy cardio — 10–15 min

This is still a gym day: keep the weights moderate and focus on clean technique.

SUNFull rest ♡
☁︎

No gym and no guilt. Eat regular meals, hydrate, sleep well and do only gentle walking if you want.

Recovery is part of muscle growth.

Progress without overdoing it

  • Start with light weights for the first 1–2 weeks to learn technique.
  • When you can complete the top end of the rep range with good form, increase the weight a little next time.
  • For muscle gain, eat enough overall food and include a protein source in each meal; avoid crash diets.
  • Monday through Saturday are training days; keep Saturday moderate if fatigue builds. Sunday is the only planned rest day.
  • If you miss a workout, continue with the next planned day—don't double up to compensate.
Progress, not perfection. ♡
